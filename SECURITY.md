# Security Policy

The ChessRealms maintainers appreciate responsible reports of security
vulnerabilities. Please help us protect users by reporting suspected
vulnerabilities privately and allowing time for investigation before public
disclosure.

## Supported versions

Unless a repository documents a different support policy, security fixes are
made for the latest released version and the default branch. Older releases
may no longer receive security updates.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for the affected repository:

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab.
2. Select **Advisories**.
3. Select **Report a vulnerability**.

If private vulnerability reporting is not available, do not include sensitive
details in a public issue. Open a minimal issue asking the maintainers for a
private reporting channel, without describing the vulnerability, exploit, or
affected users.

Do not report ordinary bugs, feature requests, or general support questions as
security vulnerabilities. Use the repository's regular issue tracker for those
reports.

## What to include

Provide enough information for the maintainers to understand and reproduce the
problem when possible:

- the affected repository, version, commit, or deployment;
- the type of vulnerability and its potential impact;
- the conditions required to reproduce it;
- clear reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any known mitigations or workarounds;
- whether the vulnerability has been disclosed elsewhere.

Avoid including real credentials, personal data, or data obtained from systems
you do not own or have permission to test.

## What to expect

The maintainers will acknowledge the report as soon as practical, investigate
its impact, and keep communication in the private advisory. If the report is
accepted, we will work with the reporter on remediation and coordinated public
disclosure. Timelines depend on severity, complexity, and maintainer
availability, so we do not promise a fixed resolution time.

We may close reports that are not reproducible, do not have a meaningful
security impact, or concern unsupported versions. When possible, we will
explain the reason.

## Responsible research

When investigating a potential vulnerability:

- test only systems and data you own or are authorized to access;
- minimize access to data and stop if you encounter personal or confidential
  information;
- do not disrupt services, degrade availability, or destroy data;
- do not use social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks;
- give the maintainers a reasonable opportunity to address the issue before
  publishing details.

Good-faith reports that follow this policy are welcome. This policy does not
authorize testing that would otherwise be unlawful or access to third-party
systems.
